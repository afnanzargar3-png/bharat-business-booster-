# 🇮🇳 Bharat Business Booster

### Your AI-Powered Business Growth Assistant for India

**Created by [TECHZMINISTER](https://www.instagram.com/techzminister/)**

Bharat Business Booster is an India-focused Claude Skill designed to help small businesses, local shop owners, entrepreneurs and online sellers improve their marketing and customer communication using AI.

From Instagram marketing to WhatsApp sales templates, promotional campaigns and business growth strategies, this Skill helps turn everyday business challenges into actionable solutions.

**Built for Indian businesses. Designed to make AI more accessible.**

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Requirements

- An active Claude account.
- Access to custom Skills.
- The Bharat Business Booster Skill ZIP package.

### Installation Guide

**Step 1 — Download the repository**

Click the green `Code` button at the top of this GitHub repository.

Select:

`Download ZIP`

**Step 2 — Prepare the Skill ZIP**

Extract the downloaded repository.

Locate the `bharat-business-booster` folder containing `SKILL.md` and its supporting files.

Create a ZIP of that Skill folder. Keep the Skill folder as the ZIP's root.

**Step 3 — Upload to Claude**

1. Open [Claude](https://claude.ai/).
2. Navigate to Customize → Skills.
3. Select Create Skill.
4. Choose Upload a Skill.
5. Upload your Skill ZIP.

**Step 4 — Enable the Skill**

Enable Bharat Business Booster in your Skills settings.

Your Skill is now ready to use!

## 🔒 Important Notes

- This is an AI-assisted marketing Skill, not an automatic business management application.
- It does not automatically publish Instagram posts.
- It does not automatically send WhatsApp messages.
